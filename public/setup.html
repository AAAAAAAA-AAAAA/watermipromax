Not working?

      
    
        
  • No POST 200: check the Wi-Fi name and password. The ESP32 cannot use 5 GHz Wi-Fi.
    • 
        
  • POST 404: the plant was deleted or the server was reset. Download the sketch again and upload it.
    • 
        
  • POST 401: the sensor key does not match the server's SENSOR_KEY.
    • 
        
  • Humidity and temperature say "Not connected": check the DHT22 data wire on GPIO 4. A bare DHT22 needs a 10 kΩ resistor between DATA and 3.3V.
    • 
        
  • Everything goes "Not connected" after 30 seconds: the board stopped sending. Check power and Wi-Fi.
